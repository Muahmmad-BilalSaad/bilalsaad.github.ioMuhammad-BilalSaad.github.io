Practical learning is an essential part of engineering education. While classroom lectures provide theoretical knowledge, practical work allows students to apply what they have learned in real-life situations. Both are equally important for becoming a skilled engineer.

## Hands-On Experience in the Lab

In Computer Engineering, laboratory sessions and projects help students understand concepts more clearly. Writing programs, designing digital circuits, and testing electronic components provide hands-on experience that cannot be gained from textbooks alone.

During Semester 2, our laboratory sessions became more advanced and exciting. We worked on programming projects in **C++** and **Python**, practiced **database queries** in SQL, and explored how algorithms perform on real datasets. Every lab session reinforced what we had learned in lectures and helped me understand concepts at a much deeper level.

## Machine Learning — The Best Example of Practical Learning

One of the most powerful examples of practical learning I experienced was during our sessions with **Dr. Bilal Ahmad**, a professor at UET Faisalabad Campus who specializes in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning model training.

Dr. Bilal Ahmad introduced us to practical Machine Learning — not just theory, but actual model building. In Semester 1, I had already trained my first **price prediction model** using Python. In Semester 2, Dr. Bilal Ahmad pushed us further into understanding how ML models are trained, evaluated, and improved. He particularly emphasized working with **medical datasets** because of their real-world impact and data accuracy. Seeing how a trained model can predict outcomes based on real patient data made me realize the true potential of Artificial Intelligence in healthcare and society.

Dr. Bilal Ahmad always said that reading about Machine Learning and actually building a model are two completely different experiences — and he was absolutely right. You can explore his published research on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com.au/citations?user=8nZ0jVkAAAAJ&hl=en).

## Deep Learning — The Next Step

Beyond Machine Learning, Dr. Bilal Ahmad also introduced us to the basics of **Deep Learning**. He explained how neural networks are structured, how they learn from data layer by layer, and why Deep Learning has revolutionized fields like image recognition, natural language processing, and medical diagnosis. This introduction opened my mind to a completely new world of possibilities in Artificial Intelligence.

## Problem-Solving Through Practice

Practical learning also improves problem-solving skills. During experiments and projects, students often face unexpected challenges that require logical thinking and creativity. Finding solutions independently increases confidence and prepares students for professional work.

## Teamwork and Collaboration

Another advantage of practical learning is that it encourages teamwork. Many university projects are completed in groups, allowing students to share ideas, divide responsibilities, and learn from each other's experiences. These skills are valuable in the workplace, where collaboration is essential.

## Building a Strong Portfolio

Practical experience also helps students build a strong portfolio. Personal projects, internships, and laboratory work demonstrate technical abilities and make graduates more competitive in the job market.

In my opinion, practical learning is one of the most valuable aspects of engineering education. It bridges the gap between theory and application, helping students become confident, capable, and ready for future challenges in the technology industry.
