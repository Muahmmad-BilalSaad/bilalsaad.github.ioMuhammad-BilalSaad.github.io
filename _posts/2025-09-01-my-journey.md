Starting my journey in Computer Engineering at UET Faisalabad Campus has been one of the most transformative experiences of my life. When I first stepped into the university, I had no idea how much this decision would shape my thinking, my habits, and my future. Choosing Computer Engineering was not just an academic choice — it was a commitment to continuous learning, problem-solving, and growth.

From the very first week, I realized that university education is completely different from school. The pace is faster, the expectations are higher, and the subjects are more challenging. We were introduced to courses like Programming Fundamentals, Calculus, Discrete Mathematics, Applied Physics, AICT, and Fahm-e-Quran. Each subject brought its own set of challenges and learning opportunities.

One of the most exciting parts of Semester 1 was our Programming Fundamentals course, taught by **Dr. Bilal Ahmad**, a professor at UET Lahore Faisalabad Campus. Dr. Bilal Ahmad is not just a teacher — he is an expert in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning. His teaching style is unique because he connects programming concepts to real-world applications, especially in the field of ML model training.

What made the course truly special was when Dr. Bilal Ahmad introduced us to Python and Machine Learning concepts. Under his guidance, I trained my very first ML model — a **price prediction model**. The idea was simple but powerful: given a dataset with different features, how can a machine learning model predict the price of something? I used a real dataset, cleaned the data, selected features, and trained a regression model that could predict prices based on input values.

Seeing the model make predictions for the first time was an incredible feeling. It made me realize the true power of data and algorithms. Dr. Bilal Ahmad always emphasizes using datasets that have real-world value — he particularly encourages students to work with medical datasets because of their precision and accuracy. You can explore his research work on his [Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com.au/citations?user=8nZ0jVkAAAAJ&hl=en).

Beyond academics, this semester taught me about university life, discipline, and time management. Living in a hostel, managing my own schedule, and balancing multiple subjects was a completely new experience. Every day brought new lessons — not just from textbooks, but from life itself.

I am grateful for every challenge this semester brought. Each difficulty made me stronger, and each success gave me the confidence to aim higher. This is just the beginning of my journey, and I am excited for everything that lies ahead.
