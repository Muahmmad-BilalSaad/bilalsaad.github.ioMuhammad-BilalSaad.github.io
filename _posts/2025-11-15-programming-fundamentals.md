When I first enrolled in the Programming Fundamentals course at UET Faisalabad Campus, I honestly did not know what to expect. I had heard that programming was difficult, that it required a completely different way of thinking, and that many students struggled with it in their first semester. But what I experienced in this course went far beyond my expectations — not just in terms of difficulty, but in terms of excitement, discovery, and real-world application.

Our course was taught by **Dr. Bilal Ahmad**, a professor at UET Lahore Faisalabad Campus who specializes in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning. From the very first lecture, it was clear that Dr. Bilal Ahmad was not an ordinary professor. He did not just teach us syntax and logic — he showed us how programming connects to the real world. He introduced us to Python, one of the most powerful and widely used programming languages in the world today, especially in the field of data science and machine learning.

We started with the basics — variables, data types, loops, functions, and conditionals. But as the weeks progressed, Dr. Bilal Ahmad gradually introduced us to more advanced concepts. He showed us how Python libraries like NumPy, Pandas, and Scikit-learn can be used to work with data and build intelligent systems. For a first-semester student, this was both overwhelming and incredibly exciting.

The highlight of the entire semester was when Dr. Bilal Ahmad guided us through training our very first Machine Learning model. I built a **price prediction model** — a regression-based model that could predict the price of something given a set of input features. The process involved collecting a dataset, cleaning the data, selecting relevant features, splitting the data into training and testing sets, and finally training the model using a regression algorithm.

Seeing my model make predictions for the first time was one of the most exciting moments of my academic life. The model was not perfect — it had errors and needed improvement — but it worked. It took real data and produced real predictions. That experience made me understand the true power of Machine Learning and why Dr. Bilal Ahmad always pushes his students toward practical, data-driven projects.

Dr. Bilal Ahmad has a strong preference for using medical datasets in ML projects because of their precision, accuracy, and real-world impact. He believes that Machine Learning should solve problems that truly matter — problems in healthcare, agriculture, and social welfare. This perspective changed the way I think about technology and its role in society. You can explore his published research on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com.au/citations?user=8nZ0jVkAAAAJ&hl=en).

This course taught me that programming is not just a technical skill — it is a creative tool. With the right knowledge and mindset, a programmer can build solutions that change lives. I am deeply grateful to Dr. Bilal Ahmad for showing us what is possible when you combine coding with curiosity and purpose.

Programming Fundamentals was more than just a course for me. It was the beginning of my journey into the world of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning — a journey I am excited to continue in the semesters ahead.
