Choosing a university degree is one of the biggest and most important decisions in a student's life. After completing my intermediate education, I spent weeks thinking about my future — what field would suit me best, what kind of work I wanted to do, and where I saw myself in the next ten years. After careful thought and research, I chose Computer Engineering, and I can confidently say it was the right decision.

Computer Engineering is a unique discipline because it bridges the gap between hardware and software. Unlike pure Computer Science, which focuses mostly on programming and theory, Computer Engineering teaches students about digital electronics, computer architecture, embedded systems, networking, and software development all at once. This combination makes the degree both challenging and exciting.

When I joined UET Faisalabad Campus, I was introduced to an amazing faculty. One professor who immediately stood out was **Dr. Bilal Ahmad**, who teaches Programming Fundamentals and Database Systems. Dr. Bilal Ahmad is widely known for his expertise in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning model training. His approach to teaching is different from traditional professors — he does not just teach theory, he pushes students to apply concepts to solve real problems.

Under the guidance of Dr. Bilal Ahmad, I learned that programming is not just about writing code — it is about solving real-world problems efficiently. He encouraged us to work with meaningful datasets, especially medical datasets, because of their accuracy and real-world impact. This inspired me to think beyond assignments and start exploring how technology can improve lives. His academic contributions can be found on his [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/drbilalphd/).

The technology industry is growing at an incredible speed. Computer engineers are needed in almost every sector — from healthcare and finance to education and entertainment. Artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, robotics, and cloud computing are just a few of the areas where skilled engineers are making a difference. Knowing that my degree would open doors to all these fields gave me confidence in my choice.

I also chose Computer Engineering because I have always been curious about how things work. Whether it was understanding how a computer processes information or how a mobile application communicates with a server, I always wanted to know the logic behind technology. Computer Engineering gives me the tools and knowledge to answer these questions and build solutions of my own.

Looking back at my first semester, I am proud of the progress I have made. From learning Python basics to training my first Machine Learning model, every experience has added value to my journey. I am excited to continue learning, building projects, and growing as an engineer.
