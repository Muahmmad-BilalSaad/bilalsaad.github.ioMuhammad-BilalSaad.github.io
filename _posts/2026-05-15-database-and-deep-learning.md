Semester 2 has been one of the most exciting and challenging periods of my university journey so far. Among all the subjects and experiences this semester brought, two stand out the most — **Database Systems** and my continued exploration of **Deep Learning** under the guidance of **Dr. Bilal Ahmad**.

## Database Systems — Understanding How Data Works

Database Systems is one of the most practical and important subjects in Computer Engineering. Before taking this course, I used to think of data as just numbers and text stored somewhere on a computer. But after studying database design, SQL queries, table relationships, normalization, and data retrieval, I now understand how the entire digital world is built on organized, structured data.

Learning **SQL** was particularly interesting. Writing queries to retrieve, filter, sort, and join data from multiple tables felt like learning a new language — one that speaks directly to computers about information. Every query I wrote successfully gave me a sense of accomplishment and deepened my understanding of how real applications like banking systems, hospital management software, and e-commerce platforms manage millions of records efficiently.

Database Systems also taught me that good data organization is the foundation of good software. No matter how powerful an algorithm is, if the data behind it is poorly structured, the results will be unreliable.

## Deep Learning — The Next Level of AI

While Database Systems gave me a strong foundation in data management, my sessions with **Dr. Bilal Ahmad** opened my eyes to the world of **Deep Learning** — the most advanced branch of Artificial Intelligence.

Dr. Bilal Ahmad is a professor at UET Faisalabad Campus who specializes in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning model training. In Semester 1, he guided me through building my first **Machine Learning price prediction model**. In Semester 2, he introduced us to **Deep Learning** — explaining how artificial neural networks are structured, how they learn from large amounts of data through forward and backward propagation, and why they are so powerful for tasks like image recognition, natural language processing, and medical diagnosis.

What fascinated me most was learning how a Deep Learning model can look at thousands of medical images and learn to detect diseases with accuracy that rivals experienced doctors. Dr. Bilal Ahmad always emphasized using **medical datasets** in AI projects because of their precision and real-world importance. He believes that AI should be used to solve problems that truly matter — problems in healthcare, agriculture, and human welfare. You can explore his published research on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com.au/citations?user=8nZ0jVkAAAAJ&hl=en).

## Connecting Database Systems With AI

One of the most important lessons I learned this semester is how **Database Systems and Machine Learning connect**. Every AI model needs data — and that data must be stored, managed, and retrieved efficiently using databases. Understanding both fields together gave me a much clearer picture of how real AI systems are built from end to end.

A complete AI project involves collecting data, storing it in a well-designed database, cleaning and preparing it, training a Machine Learning or Deep Learning model, and finally deploying that model to serve real users. This end-to-end understanding is what separates a student who only knows theory from an engineer who can build real systems.
