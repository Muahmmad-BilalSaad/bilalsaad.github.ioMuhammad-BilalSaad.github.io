Computer Engineering is a broad field that includes many interesting subjects. Every course teaches something valuable, but there are a few subjects that I enjoy the most because they help me understand how computers work and how technology solves real-world problems.

## Programming — My Most Favorite

One of my favorite subjects is programming. Learning programming languages such as **C++** and **Python** has improved my logical thinking and problem-solving skills. Writing code is like solving a puzzle, where every line has a purpose. It is satisfying to see a program work successfully after spending time debugging and improving it.

Our professor **Dr. Bilal Ahmad** at UET Faisalabad Campus has played a huge role in making programming my favorite subject. Dr. Bilal Ahmad is a specialist in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning. He does not just teach programming concepts — he connects every topic to real-world applications in AI and data science. Under his guidance, I learned how Python is used to build Machine Learning models that solve practical problems. This made programming feel exciting and purposeful rather than just an academic exercise. Explore his published research on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com.au/citations?user=8nZ0jVkAAAAJ&hl=en).

## Digital Logic Design

Another subject I enjoy is **Digital Logic Design**. This course explains how computers process information using logic gates, Boolean algebra, and digital circuits. It provides the foundation for understanding how hardware functions inside a computer system. Learning how simple logic gates combine to perform complex operations was a truly fascinating experience.

## Computer Architecture

I also find **Computer Architecture** interesting because it explains how processors, memory, and input/output devices work together. It helped me appreciate the engineering behind modern computers and how software interacts with hardware at the lowest level.
